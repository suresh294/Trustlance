Blockchain-based escrow protects both clients and freelancers by holding payment in a smart contract instead of a company or bank account. When a client posts a job, their payment is locked immediately, so the freelancer knows funds genuinely exist before starting work. Once the freelancer submits their completed work, an automated compliance check evaluates it, and the smart contract releases payment automatically if the work meets the agreed standard. If a dispute arises, the funds remain safely locked rather than being controlled by either party alone, and both sides can review the same verifiable evidence. This removes the need to trust a centralized platform, reduces payment delays, and ensures freelancers are paid fairly for work that genuinely meets the client's requirements.
