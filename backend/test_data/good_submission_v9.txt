Blockchain-based escrow safeguards both clients and freelancers during payment disputes by securely keeping the payment locked within a smart contract. Neither party can withdraw the funds until the completed work has been properly reviewed and approved. This prevents clients from paying for unfinished or undelivered work while also ensuring freelancers are not left without payment after completing their tasks. If a dispute occurs, the escrow system keeps the funds securely locked instead of favoring either party, allowing both the client and freelancer a fair opportunity to resolve the issue.
