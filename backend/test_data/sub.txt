Blockchain-based escrow protects both clients and freelancers by securely holding payment funds in a smart contract until the agreed work is reviewed and approved. Clients are protected because they do not have to release payment before receiving satisfactory work. Freelancers are also protected because the payment is already locked and reserved for them once the agreement is funded. If a dispute occurs, the escrow prevents either party from unfairly accessing the funds immediately, allowing the disagreement to be resolved through the platform's defined process.
